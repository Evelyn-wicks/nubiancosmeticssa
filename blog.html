Skincare Tips

Skincare Tips and tricks on how to prepare your skin before applying makeup.

Check out our basic guide for beginner makeup toutorials!

Learn how to take care of your skin and avoide brakouts with our expert advise. skincare before makeup is essential because it creates smooth, hydrated canvas, helps makeup last longer, and protects your skin from irritation or clogged pores. Without proper prep, makeup can look patchy, cakey or even fade quickly

Why Skincare Matters?

  • Hydration: Moisturizing your skin ensures it stays hydrated, preventing dryness and flakiness that can make makeup look uneven.
  • Longevity: Properly prepped skin allows makeup to adhere better, helping it last throughout the day without fading or smudging.
  • Protection: A good skincare routine creates a barrier that protects your skin from potential irritation caused by makeup products.
